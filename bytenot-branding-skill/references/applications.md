# Applications: what the guidelines themselves make

Source: guidelines pages 47-61. These are the only worked examples of the system, so treat them
as the reference for how it looks when used well. The pages are described in `guideline-pages.md`.

## The pattern that repeats

Across every example:

- The field is **flat Orange or flat Maroon**, sometimes Mint or Milk White for stationery.
- **One text colour** per field: Mint on Maroon, Maroon or Mint on Orange, Orange on a Maroon
  block.
- The **logo is one colour** on Orange and Maroon fields (all Mint, or all Maroon on Orange).
- Headlines are the brand's own lines, in sentence case, aligned left, a few words per line.
- A **square-built device** finishes the layout: a pattern band, a block, a symbol in a square, or
  a stepped photograph.
- Contact details sit small in a corner: `www.bytenot.com` over `info@bytenot.com`.

Three things in these mock-ups go against the rule pages, and the rule pages win:

- On the vertical posters the wordmark and the contact lines run up the side. Page 22 says do not
  rotate. Do not copy it; keep the logo upright.
- One social post (p58) centres its headline. Page 37 says always align to the left.
- One poster capitalises each line ("We are / Here to / Close / The gap"). Page 36 says sentence
  case.

## Recipes

**Statement on a field.** Maroon or Orange ground. Logo top left in one colour, contact top right.
Tagline as a Subheading, left aligned, two lines. Bottom third: a stepped photograph or a pattern
band.

**Block in a field.** Orange ground with a Maroon square in one corner (or the reverse). The
words go inside the square in the display colour for that square. The symbol sits in the opposite
corner.

**Photo with a bar.** A real photograph filling the top. A Maroon bar across the foot: an Orange
square with the Maroon symbol at its left end, then the line in Mint.

**Avatar and cover.** Avatar: the bug, or the Orange symbol on a Maroon disc. Cover: Maroon, all
Mint logo, tagline, squares breaking out of one corner.

**Stationery.** Milk White sheet, Maroon text. An Orange band on one edge and a Maroon band on
the adjacent one, with a Mint square and Maroon symbol where they meet.
