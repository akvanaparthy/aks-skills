# Guideline pages, described

These describe pages of Bytenot's brand guidelines PDF by page number, in place of page images.

Common to every page: the PDF's own page ground is Mint Chiffon `#E9FFEF`; titles and body copy
are Maroon Oak `#4D1B1E`, set in Gabarito, aligned left; the section name sits small in Grey at
top right with the page number beside it (applications pages put both at the bottom). Example
panels drawn "white" are Milk White `#FCFCFC`. In the PDF's images the light ink on Orange and
Maroon reads as Mint; the owner's ruling (SKILL.md) is Milk White on Maroon, Mint on Orange.

## p18: Minimum size

- Rule: use judgment when scaling, but never go below these sizes.
- **Logo** (symbol + wordmark): minimum height **20px**.
- **Wordmark** alone: minimum height **20px**.
- The symbol and the bug are shown too; the left-hand captions for them repeat the clear-space
  wording ("equivalent to the height and width of the symbol / bug") rather than giving a pixel
  size. No minimum size in px is printed for symbol or bug.
- Four Milk White panels in a 2x2 grid, each with thin Maroon-ish guide lines boxing the mark:
  - Top left: full logo, Orange symbol + Maroon wordmark; horizontal guides at the logo's top and
    bottom, vertical guides at its left and right edges.
  - Top right: Maroon wordmark alone, guides at cap/baseline height and its left/right edges.
  - Bottom left: Orange symbol alone, guides hugging it.
  - Bottom right: the bug (Orange circle with the symbol knocked out in Milk White), guides
    hugging the circle.

## p19: Clear space

- Rule: do not crowd the identity; these are minimum spacing metrics.
- **Logo**: clear space = the symbol's **height** (above and below) and the symbol's **width**
  (left and right).
- **Wordmark**: clear space = the wordmark's **cap height** on every side.
- **Symbol**: clear space = the symbol's height and width.
- **Bug**: clear space = the bug's height and width.
- Diagrams (2x2, Milk White panels, light grey grid): each mark sits in a light grey box padded
  by the stated unit.
  - Logo: copies of the symbol, ghosted in pale grey, sit in the four corner cells to show the
    unit (one symbol-width to the sides, one symbol-height above and below).
  - Wordmark: plain grey corner squares one cap-height in size.
  - Symbol: ghosted symbols in the corners, one symbol each way.
  - Bug: grey circles the size of the bug in the four corners.
- Marks shown: Orange symbol + Maroon wordmark; Maroon wordmark; Orange symbol; Orange bug.

## p21: Logo and colour (logo section)

- Rule: keep enough contrast between mark and ground. On dark grounds use the logo mainly in
  light colours; on light grounds mainly in dark. Refers to the colour section for detail.
- Four approved versions, 2x2 panels:
  - Top left: **Orange ground**, whole logo (symbol + wordmark) in the light colour (Mint).
  - Top right: **Milk White ground**, Orange symbol + Maroon wordmark (the primary logo).
  - Bottom left: **Maroon ground**, Orange symbol + light wordmark (Mint in the image; Milk White
    by the owner's ruling).
  - Bottom right: **Orange ground**, whole logo in Maroon (symbol and wordmark).
- Logo is centred in each panel and small relative to it (roughly a third of the panel width).

## p22: Logo misuse

- Heading idea: do not diminish the identity. Eight crossed-out examples, each on Milk White with
  a red diagonal strike line and a red X:
- Do not stretch or distort (logo squashed vertically, widened horizontally).
- Do not use unapproved colours (symbol and wordmark both Orange).
- Do not outline (symbol and wordmark drawn as thin outlines, no fill).
- Do not modify logo colour applications (Maroon symbol + Orange wordmark, i.e. colours swapped).
- Do not change logo placement (wordmark first, symbol to its right; both Orange).
- Do not apply effects (Orange symbol + Maroon wordmark with a drop shadow/glow).
- Do not use low contrast combinations (pale pink symbol + pale grey wordmark on Milk White).
- Do not rotate (logo tilted roughly 20-25 degrees anticlockwise).

## p25: Primary colour

- Rule: these values are the core palette, used most often, and come first whenever the palette
  must be limited. Opulent Orange is the signature colour that sets Bytenot apart.
- Swatch block, left to right: a tall Orange column `#EB6140` (hex label in Milk White), a tall
  Maroon column `#4D1B1E` (label Milk White), then a column split in three stacked bands: Mint
  Chiffon `#E9FFEF` (with a thin Maroon outline so it shows on the Mint page), Milk White
  `#FCFCFC`, Grey `#BFBFBF` (labels in Maroon).
- Orange and Maroon columns are equal width (about a quarter of the block each); the light column
  is about half the block wide. Five primary colours in all.

## p26: Extended palette (colour values)

- Page title is "Extended Palette". Rule: the extended palette adds flexibility; apply it
  consistently across marketing and channels to build recognition.
- Four large swatches, 2x2, each with a value list at bottom left:
  - **Opulent Orange**: CMYK 0, 59, 73, 8; RGB 235, 97, 64; HEX `#EB6140`; Pantone 2060 C.
    Label text Maroon.
  - **Maroon Oak**: CMYK 0, 65, 61, 70; RGB 77, 27, 30; HEX `#4D1B1E`; Pantone 4102 C.
    Label text Milk White.
  - **Mint Chiffon**: CMYK 9, 0, 6, 0; RGB 233, 255, 239; HEX `#E9FFEF`; Pantone 621 C.
    Thin Maroon outline; label text Maroon.
  - **Milk White**: CMYK 0, 0, 0, 1; RGB 252, 252, 252; HEX `#FCFCFC`; Pantone 663 C.
    Label text Maroon.
- Grey and the three tints/shades are not given CMYK/RGB/Pantone on this page.

- Eight square tiles, 4 across x 2 rows, each with the symbol centred and its hex at bottom left:
  - Orange `#EB6140`: symbol **Mint**; hex label light.
  - Orange tint `#F8CABE`: symbol **Orange**; label Maroon.
  - Maroon `#4D1B1E`: symbol **Orange**; label light.
  - Maroon tint `#ECCACC`: symbol **Maroon**; label Maroon.
  - Mint Chiffon `#E9FFEF` (Maroon outline): symbol **Orange**; label Maroon.

## p28: Type and colour

- Rule: strong contrast is essential for legibility and accessibility. The combinations are
  complementary, bold and accessible, designed to work together, and should be used as intended.
- Same eight tiles in the same order as p27, each with a large "Aa" in Gabarito (Medium/Bold),
  centred; no hex labels:
  - Orange: **Mint** text.
  - Orange tint: **Orange** text.
  - Maroon: **Orange** text.
  - Maroon tint: **Maroon** text.
  - Mint Chiffon (outlined): **Orange** text.
  - Mint shade: **Mint** text.
  - Milk White: **Orange** text.
  - Grey: **Milk White** text.
- Note: the Orange-tint, Mint-shade and Grey tiles are visibly low contrast despite the rule (see
  SKILL.md, "ornament only").

## p29: Colour misuse

- Heading idea: do not diminish the value of colour. Six crossed-out panels (red diagonal, red X):
- Do not use unapproved colours (flat lavender/periwinkle field, about `#988BE0`).
- Do not use gradients (black to white horizontal gradient).
- Do not use low contrast combinations ("We are here to close the gap" in Mint shade/greyish
  green on Orange).
- Do not use the logo on colours outside the palette (Orange symbol + Maroon wordmark on a sage
  green); in that situation use the **black or white** logo.
- Do not use multiple text colours at once (Maroon ground; first line Orange, second line Mint
  shade).
- Do not modify logo colour applications (Maroon symbol + Orange wordmark on Milk White).

## p33: Weights in use

- Rule: weight separates levels of information and builds hierarchy; use only the approved
  weights listed.
- Two specimens, separated by thin grey rules, all in Maroon on the Mint page:
  - **Gabarito Regular**: A-Z, a-z, 0-9 and `.,&?!`.
  - **Gabarito Medium**: the same character set.
- Only Regular and Medium appear here; Bold is used for Headings on p36 (see SKILL.md).

## p36: Hierarchy

- Rule: size, scale and placement shape understanding; keep a clear, intentional difference
  between type sizes.
- Three rows, specs on the left, sample on the right, all Maroon, aligned left:
  - **Heading**: Gabarito Bold, sentence case, 144pt, leading 129.6pt (90%), tracking 30.
    Sample: "Worry not / about your tech" on two lines, very large.
  - **Subheading**: Gabarito Medium, sentence case, 60pt, leading printed as 48pt (100%),
    tracking -20. Sample: "We are here to / close the gap". (48pt is not 100% of 60pt; see
    SKILL.md.)
  - **Body**: Gabarito Regular, sentence case, 18pt, leading 23.4pt (130%), tracking -10.
    Sample: a seven-line paragraph about the studio, its founders and its promise ("byte for
    the technology, not for the worry"), set about 600px wide.

## p37: Type misuse

- Heading idea: do not diminish the identity. Six crossed-out panels, Maroon text on Milk White:
- Do not set in all caps ("WORRY NOT / ABOUT YOUR TECH").
- Do not set in all lowercase ("worry not / about your tech").
- Do not use unapproved typefaces (same line in a serif).
- Do not use incorrect leading (lines spread far apart).
- Do not use incorrect tracking (letters squeezed so they touch on line two).
- Do not align text incorrectly; always align left (example is right-aligned).

## p47: Patterns

- Defines a brand pattern as a repeating motif made for the brand, part of the identity with
  logo, colour, type and imagery. Three square pattern tiles, each a 9x9 grid of square cells:
  - Left and right (the same pattern, "tall-short"): **Orange on Mint**. Columns 2, 4, 6, 8 hold
    Orange bars two cells tall; columns 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 hold single Orange squares on the rows
    between the bars (rows 3, 6, 9); everything else is the Mint ground. Reads as stacked rows of
    tall bars separated by a checker row.
  - Middle ("two-tone"): **Orange and Maroon**, no ground showing. Each row runs
    1 Orange, 1 Maroon, 2 Orange, 1 Maroon, 2 Orange, 1 Maroon, 1 Orange (cells); the next row
    swaps Orange and Maroon. Nine alternating rows give a checker with double-width bars.
- Cells are hard-edged squares here (the owner allows the patterns as drawn, as one device).
